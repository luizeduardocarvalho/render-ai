02

Capabilities

Six
  1. 2.1

    Mask-driven placement

    Paint a region, assign a real product from your library, and render-ai places that exact item - matching perspective and light.

  2. 2.2

    Photoreal in one call

    A high-fidelity model renders up to 4K; switch to a faster model for quick, low-cost comparisons.

  3. 2.3

    Multi-angle consistency

    Pin any render as a style anchor so every other view of the space stays coherent and on-brand across angles.

  4. 2.4

    Preservation checks

    Automatic edge-IoU and object-inventory diffs flag when a render drifts from your original geometry - nothing sneaks in.

  5. 2.5

    Cost & metrics up front

    Per-render latency, token usage, and an estimated cost - so you always know what a render costs before you commit.

  6. 2.6

    Local & private

    Runs on your machine, in memory. No cloud storage, no database - your screenshots and products never leave your session.

03

Method

Four steps
  1. 01

    Upload a screenshot

    Drop in a SketchUp screenshot of any view. render-ai reads its exact resolution.

  2. 02

    Mask & assign

    Paint the regions to replace and assign each one a real product from your asset library.

  3. 03

    Set the scene

    Choose scene, lighting, materials, and any extra direction - interior or exterior.

  4. 04

    Render photoreal

    Generate up to four variations, keep the best, and reuse it as the anchor for other angles.
